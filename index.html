Road safety for motorcyclists · Barcelona

Vision Zero BCN

Fewer motorcyclists killed or injured in Barcelona. We analyse every crash in the city’s open data, propose solutions, send each one to whoever can act on it, and learn from every new report.

The problem

Every year thousands of riders crash and more than a hundred are killed or seriously injured

~5,000motorcycle crashes a year
~119crashes a year with a rider killed or seriously injured (KSI)
~10riders killed a year: 109 between 2015 and 2025
138serious injuries in 2025, above the 2019 level (123)
Riders killedRiders seriously injured

Source: Guàrdia Urbana de Barcelona, Open Data BCN · 55,523 geolocated motorcycle and moped crashes, 2015–2025.

Deaths are the headline, but about ten a year are too few to target statistically on their own. We work on KSI and on crash volume: that is how deaths come down.

The insight

Fixing black spots is necessary, but most of the harm happens elsewhere

82%of rider deaths happened outside the 382 black spots
76%of killed-or-seriously-injured crashes did too

So we work at two levels: we treat the black spots, and we attack the risk patterns that repeat across the whole network.

Black spots: density clusters of at least 20 crashes within 20 m, 2015–2025. They concentrate 23% of all rider crashes.

The platform

From crash data to action, continuously

Analyse

Places (382 black spots found with density clustering) and patterns across the network: who, when, what kind of crash.

Propose

A rule engine with explainable machine learning (SHAP) turns each pattern into concrete measures, with the evidence behind them.

Route

Each measure goes to who can act on it: engineers, police, fleets or riders, as a work order or a brief.

Learn

New crash reports and measured results retrain the models, so the next proposal is better.

Work orders use standard specifications: skid resistance SRT ≥ 65 (UNE-EN 1436), D400 covers (EN 124), pendulum testing (UNE-EN 13036-4), amber and all-red signal timings, lighting class CE2 (UNE-EN 13201-2).

Who acts

A system that learns

With the Guàrdia Urbana, every report makes the next proposal better

1 · New data

Police crash reports, rider near-miss reports, fleet telemetry.

2 · Retrain

Models update; a new version replaces the old one only if it scores better on data it has never seen.

3 · Propose

Fresh recommendations, routed to each owner.

4 · Measure

Before–after results of each measure become evidence for the next round.

The knowledge base grows like a snowball: the longer it runs, the better it knows which measure works where.

Near-miss capture and a space-time anomaly detector are built and tested on synthetic patterns; validating them with real riders is part of the pilot.

Why trust us

We test our own claims, and publish what does not work

Temporal validation only

Models train on the past and are scored on years they never saw.

Honest baselines

In a 2015–2019 → 2020–2025 backtest our ML ranking did not yet beat a simple crash count, so we lead with diagnosis and routing, not prediction.

The right target

About ten deaths a year are too few to predict where; we target KSI and crashes, where the signal is.
